Personal & household assistants

A little less to keep in your head.

Holly is a privately operated assistant, built on Hermes Agent, for everyday personal organisation and shared household administration.

What Holly does

Depending on the features enabled for a particular assistant, Holly can review relevant emails, help prepare for calendar events, track orders and follow-ups, and organise selected household information.

This information site covers the Holly Personal and Holly Household variants. It is not a public sign-up service or a login portal.

Holly Household uses authorised shared Google Calendar information for household events and preparation. Email-review features apply only to variants where they are actually enabled and authorised; a granted permission alone does not establish that a feature is in use.

Connecting a Google account

An account holder grants Google access through Google's authorisation screen. The actual permissions requested are shown there. An assistant must be separately configured and authorised for the account and features it uses.

Using the same information site does not grant access to another person's mailbox, calendar or private files. Shared household information is governed by the relevant account permissions, sharing settings and household agreements.

The Google application name may include the relevant personal or household variant. This site describes the common integration; it does not mean that every variant uses every feature.

What happens to information

Relevant information may be processed by the configured AI provider and retained in the operator's assistant records or private document storage. The privacy policy explains these data flows, the account holder’s controls, and the limits of any provider-specific assurances.

For support or privacy questions, use the operator's support email displayed on the relevant Google authorisation screen. Do not put private account information in a public GitHub issue.
