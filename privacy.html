Privacy policy

This policy describes the common data flows for privately operated Holly Personal and Holly Household assistants built on Hermes Agent. Features and account access differ by assistant. It does not authorise new access or replace an account holder's consent.

1. Google information accessed

When an account holder authorises the relevant integration, the assistant may access Google data needed for its enabled features. This can include:

  • Email headers, content, attachments and relevant sent-thread context for email review, order tracking, document filing and follow-ups.
  • Calendar lists, event times, locations and relevant descriptions for scheduling context, preparation and agreed calendar assistance.

The permissions shown on Google's consent screen determine the account-level grant. An assistant's operating permissions may be narrower than that grant. A variant without an enabled email feature must not be described as reviewing email simply because this common policy includes it.

For the Household variant, this policy describes authorised shared Google Calendar use for events and preparation. It does not assert that Household email review is enabled or that it has access to any person's entire private diary.

Personal variants may also differ: calendar use is established for more than one personal deployment, but email review has not been established for every personal deployment. Installed tools, requested permissions and currently granted permissions are different things; none alone establishes actual use of a Google service.

2. Purpose and use

Information is used to provide the account holder's or household's configured assistant features, such as preparing summaries, identifying relevant actions, maintaining selected records and preparing drafts. Access to one person's data does not itself permit sharing it with another person.

This information website does not create, broaden or merge Google authorisations. Changes to mail, calendar entries or other services remain subject to the relevant assistant's agreed action permissions.

3. AI processing and service providers

Selected information may be transmitted to the configured AI service to produce the assistant's responses or perform an authorised task. It is not accurate to describe all processing as exclusively local.

Provider identity, retention and data-use settings depend on the service and account configuration. The operator must confirm that the selected service's handling of Google data is consistent with Google's applicable user-data and Limited Use requirements before enabling it.

Current deployments use OpenAI Codex for relevant model processing. For personal ChatGPT plans, OpenAI documents that its Improve the model for everyone control also applies to Codex tasks; a separate Codex Include environments setting can control additional environment context. Those controls must be reviewed and training/data-sharing disabled for Google-connected processing where required by Google’s policies. Publication of this policy does not confirm an individual account’s settings, promise zero retention, or replace that configuration check.

See OpenAI’s data-control guidance and the Google API Services User Data Policy. The operator must not enable Google-data processing with a provider whose terms or settings are incompatible with those requirements.

4. Storage and retention

Selected message or event evidence, summaries, task state, attachments and tracker records may be retained in the operator's local assistant storage and configured private document storage, including Nextcloud where enabled. Authorised task-specific outputs may also be saved to connected household services such as Home Assistant or Notion. This is not an assertion that all Google information is automatically archived to Nextcloud or another service. The operator's private systems are not published through this website.

Retained records may remain until removed by the operator or under the configured retention settings. Revoking Google access stops future access through that authorisation but does not automatically erase previously saved copies. An account holder can request review or deletion of retained information through the relevant operator.

5. Disclosure and separation

The integration is intended for personal and household assistance, not advertising or the sale of Google account data. Information may be processed by the AI, storage and other task-specific services actually used for an authorised feature.

Sharing the homepage and privacy-policy URL does not share tokens, mailboxes or calendars. Access to household records depends on the relevant sharing settings and permissions; personal information is not made household information by this policy.

6. Account-holder controls

An account holder can review or remove the application's access through Google Account connections. Requests to change enabled features or review retained records should be made to the operator. The assistant may require a fresh authorisation after access is removed or Google invalidates it.

7. This information website

These information pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts or application cookies. GitHub Pages provides the hosting and may process visitor connection data, including IP addresses, for its own operational and security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website must never contain private Google content or OAuth credentials.

8. Contact and changes

The operator contact is the support email shown on the relevant Google application consent screen. Do not submit private content or credentials in public repository discussions or issues.

Effective date: 2026-11-08. Material processing changes require this policy and the affected assistant configuration to be reviewed together.
